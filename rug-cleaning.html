7-Step Green Cleaning Process

When you bring a beautiful rug into your home, you want it to look its best for years to come. At Noor Oriental Rugs, we believe proper cleaning is an important part of preserving a rug’s beauty, quality, and longevity.

Every rug is unique, so we carefully tailor our cleaning process to its materials, condition, and individual needs. We use a comprehensive, environmentally conscious cleaning approach inspired by traditional Persian methods passed down through generations.

Our seven-step process includes:

  1. Thorough Dusting – We carefully remove deeply embedded dirt, dust, and debris that can dull a rug's appearance and gradually wear down its fibers.
  2. Color Testing and Gentle Hand Washing – We test the colors for colorfastness before soaking and hand-washing the rug with a gentle, biodegradable shampoo. We then rinse it thoroughly and allow it to air-dry.
  3. Careful Stain Treatment – We address stains by hand and apply a stain inhibitor to help protect the fibers against future staining.
  4. Deodorizing and Disinfecting – We use non-toxic solutions to help eliminate unwanted odors and disinfect the rug.
  5. Cotton Foundation Treatment – We treat the cotton foundation to help protect against fungal growth and dry rot.
  6. Mothproofing – We apply mothproofing treatment to wool and silk fibers to help protect them from moth damage.
  7. Fiber Conditioning and Rejuvenation – We moisturize and recondition wool fibers to help restore their natural luster, softness, and beauty while supporting their longevity.
