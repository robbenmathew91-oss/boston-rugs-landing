Donate Your Rugs: Why, How, and FAQs

By Mohammad (Mo) Nooraee · November 5, 2026 · 2 min read

Why Donate Instead of Sell Your Fine Rugs?

If you have a fine handmade Oriental or Persian rug that is no longer needed, you might consider selling it. However, the process of selling a high-value rug is often complex and time-consuming. It requires obtaining a certified appraisal, arranging transportation, finding a physical showroom, or listing it on consignment without a guaranteed timeframe for sale.

Furthermore, listing expensive rugs on public classified sites like Craigslist or Facebook Marketplace introduces significant privacy and home security risks, as you must invite strangers to your home to view the rug. Donating your rug to a qualified charity is a safe, efficient, and community-focused alternative.

How Noor Oriental Rugs Simplifies Your Donation

We manage the entire lifecycle of the rug donation to make it as smooth as possible for the donor:

  • Free Pick-Up Logistics: We provide a free collection service using our company van for local donors in the Cambridge and Boston areas. For non-local or nationwide donors, we coordinate and cover UPS ground shipping services.
  • Mandatory Wash and Restoration: Recipient charities typically require donated items to be in clean, usable condition. Noor washes and performs necessary fringe or pile restoration on all donated rugs. The service cost is covered by the donor, which directly increases the rug's certified appraised value.
  • Certified Appraisals: Mo Nooraee, a Harvard Law School alumnus and master rug expert, provides certified tax appraisals for a standard fee of $350 per rug. Donors can also choose to supply their own certified third-party appraisals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What tax documentation do I receive?

For tax-deductible donations, Noor legibly completes IRS Form 8283, including Section I (description), Section II (appraiser declaration), and Section IV (recipient non-profit acknowledgment). We coordinate with the charity to secure their signature and return the completed form to you in time for your tax filings.

How long does the donation process take?

Because matching a rug to a specific charity's space and needs requires careful coordination, along with the cleaning and restoration phase, the complete process typically takes at least one year. Noor does not provide tax or legal advice, so we encourage donors to consult their tax specialists.

Is the donation reversible?

No. Once the donor and Noor enter into an agreement and the rug is processed, the donation is legally binding and irrevocable. The rug becomes the property of the receiving charity or is liquidated to forward 100% of the net proceeds to them.
