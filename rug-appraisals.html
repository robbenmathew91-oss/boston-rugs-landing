Rug appraisal in Cambridge, MA: certified evaluations by Harvard Law School alumnus Mo Nooraee. Compliant with IRS Form 8283 for high-value tax deductions.

Expert and Legally Binding Evaluations

Determining the authentic market value of a hand-knotted Oriental or Persian rug requires extensive scholarship and market experience. Led by Harvard Law alumnus and master weaver Mohammad "Mo" Nooraee, Noor Oriental Rugs, Inc. provides certified, legally binding appraisals accepted by all major insurance companies, estate executors, and the IRS. For charitable donations over $500, we manage IRS Form 8283 completion and recipient signatures.

Our appraisals include exhaustive documentation of origin, age, weaving style, dimensions, materials, and condition metrics, giving you absolute protection and accurate paper trails.

Antique and Persian Rug Appraisals for Insurance, Estates and Donations

Antique, Persian and Oriental rug appraisals are needed for insurance coverage, estate distribution, and tax deductions on donated rugs worth more than $500 (IRS Form 8283). Noor Oriental Rugs serves clients in Cambridge, Boston and across Greater Boston from our showroom on Concord Ave.

Rug Appraisal FAQs

How long does a rug appraisal take?

How does an Oriental rug appraisal work?

What does the appraisal document include?

What is a certified rug appraisal used for?

A certified rug appraisal is required for insurance coverage, estate distribution, and tax deductions on donations over $500 (IRS Form 8283).

Can you appraise a rug I want to donate?

Yes. For donated rugs worth more than $500, the IRS requires a certified appraiser to complete Section III of Form 8283, and Mo Nooraee provides certified appraisals for donors. Read more in our rug donation guide.
