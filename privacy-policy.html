Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026 · Updated November 6, 2026 (Google Analytics, Google Maps)

This policy explains what personal information Noor Oriental Rugs, Inc. ("Noor Oriental Rugs", "we", "us") collects through noororientalrugs.com and when you contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain: we collect only what we need to answer you and serve you, and we never sell your information.

Who we are

Noor Oriental Rugs, Inc., 769 Concord Ave, Cambridge, MA 02138. Phone 617-868-6667, email info@noororientalrugs.com.

Information you give us

  • Contact and consultation form: your name, email address, phone number, the type of request you choose, your message, and the page you sent it from.
  • Email, phone and visits: whatever you choose to share when you email or call us or visit the showroom, including photos of your rug.
  • When you do business with us (a purchase, cleaning, restoration, appraisal or donation): the details needed to provide the service, such as your address for pick-up or delivery, information about your rug, and payment and receipt records.

Information collected automatically

  • Google Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site: which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors come from (city or region level), what device and browser they use, how they found us, and whether they send an enquiry, tap our phone number or click our email address. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technology for this. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not see who you are. You can block it with your browser's cookie settings or with Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • No advertising trackers. We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting pixels or social-media trackers.
  • Server logs. Like every website, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when pages are requested, such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. It is used to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Fonts and icons. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and icons from cdnjs (Cloudflare). Your browser contacts those services directly, so they receive your IP address and browser information under their own privacy policies.
  • Maps. The contact section of our homepage shows an embedded Google Map of our showroom. When that part of the page loads, your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address and browser information and may set cookies under Google's privacy policy. Address and "Get directions" links open Google Maps only if you click them.

How we use your information

  • To answer your enquiry and arrange appointments, evaluations, pick-ups and deliveries.
  • To provide the services and purchases you ask for, and to keep business, tax and appraisal records.
  • To coordinate a rug donation when you ask us to, including the documentation you request.
  • To keep the website working and secure, and to understand which pages and services visitors are interested in so we can improve the site.
  • To send news or offers only if you have asked to receive them. Every such email includes a way to unsubscribe.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that run our website and email: Formspree (delivers contact-form messages to our inbox), Vercel (website hosting), Google (Google Analytics and Google Search Console), Microsoft 365 (our email), and, if you sign up for news, our email newsletter provider. They process information on our behalf to provide those services.
  • With people you ask us to involve, for example a charity receiving a rug you donate.
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, our customers or the public.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and customer and service records for as long as needed for the service and for our legal, tax and accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see below); we will do so unless we are required to keep it.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop sending you news and offers. Email info@noororientalrugs.com or call 617-868-6667. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

Security

The website is served only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and we use reasonable measures to protect the information you give us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please do not send payment card numbers or other sensitive financial information through the contact form or by email.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information: Noor Oriental Rugs, Inc., 769 Concord Ave, Cambridge, MA 02138 · 617-868-6667 · info@noororientalrugs.com.
